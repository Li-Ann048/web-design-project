Meet the Characters

The wonderful friends of Mr. Bee's world

01

Mr. Bee

Having no memory of where he came from, Mr. Bee sets off on a grand adventure to discover his roots and explore the world. His journey begins in France, where fate brings him together with his two future best friends: Mr. Octopus and Fruit Bunny.

🌍 Adventurous 💛 Kind-hearted 📖 Curious

02

Mr. Octopus

As Mr. Bee’s trusted best friend and a fearless explorer in his own right, Mr. Octopus has already mapped every hidden corner of the deep blue sea. Now, he is bringing his love for discovery to the shore, continuing his grand adventures on land.

💛 Brave ⭐ Natural Leader 🌊 Sea Lover

03

Fruit Bunny

Mr. Bee's other best friend, Fruit Bunny, appears shy at first glance, but she is a deeply sweet girl at heart. She joined the grand adventure specifically to conquer her shyness, and in the process, gained two incredible best friends.

🍒 Berries Lover 🍀 Nature Lover 🌙 Shy & Soft

04

Pho Special

A mysterious bunny whom the crew encountered during their travels. While very little is known about his origins, one thing is certain: he has journeyed to countless corners of the globe, gathering a wealth of wisdom from every single trip!

✨ Mysterious 🌍 Adventurous 📖 Knowledgeable

05

Tiny Hedgehog

A dear new friend of Mr. Bee's — Tiny Hedgehog shares his love of the quiet, cosy things in life. Every so often, the two gather together for an afternoon tea, settling in with warm cups and fresh pastries to catch up and enjoy each other's company.

🌱 Tea Enthusiast 🥐 Pastry Lover 🍄 Descreet
